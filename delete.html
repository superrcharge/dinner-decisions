Delete your data

For the Dinner Decisions app at dinnerdecisions.app

Dinner Decisions has no accounts, so there is nothing to close. Everything your family stores can be deleted from inside the app, by anyone in the family, at any time.

Pick the one that fits:

  • Delete your own data from one phone
  • Take one phone out of a family
  • Remove a name from a family
  • Delete a whole family and everything in it
  • Lost access? Ask us to do it

Delete your own data from one phone

  1. Open Dinner Decisions on that phone.
  2. Tap your name at the top of the screen. If nobody has been chosen yet, it says Choose…
  3. Tap Delete My Data. It is also in the Manage tab, under Delete.
  4. Read what will be deleted, and tap Delete My Data.

What is deleted: the phone is taken off the family's list of phones; the name the phone was picking as is removed from the family, with every vote made under it; everything the app keeps on the phone is cleared; and the phone's anonymous sign-in is deleted.

What stays: the family's meals, ingredients, other names and their votes, because everyone else still uses them. To delete those as well, delete the whole family, as described below. Coming back needs the invite code, and starts as a new phone.

Take one phone out of a family

  1. Open Dinner Decisions on that phone.
  2. Tap your name at the top of the screen. If nobody has been chosen yet, it says Choose…
  3. Under Your family, tap Leave.
  4. Tap Leave on this phone.

What is deleted: the phone is taken off the family's list of phones, and the copy of the family's lists kept on the phone is cleared. The phone can no longer see or change anything in the family.

What stays: the family's meals, names, votes and plan, because everyone else still uses them. Your name stays on the family's list until it is removed, as described next. Coming back needs the invite code.

Remove a name from a family

  1. Open Dinner Decisions on any phone in the family.
  2. Tap Manage at the bottom of the screen, and enter the Manage code if it asks for one.
  3. Under Who's in the house, tap Remove next to the name.

What is deleted: the name, from the family's list of who is picking.

What stays: votes already made under that name stay with the family's weekly history until the family is deleted. To remove those as well, delete the family, or email us and we will remove them for you.

Delete a whole family and everything in it

  1. Open Dinner Decisions on any phone in the family.
  2. Tap Manage at the bottom of the screen, and enter the Manage code if it asks for one.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Manage tab and tap Delete this family.
  4. Type the family's name exactly as it appears, to confirm.
  5. Tap Delete for everyone.

What is deleted, straight away and for everyone: the family's name, every meal and ingredient, every suggestion, every name, every vote and weekly plan, the invite code, and the family's list of phones. Every other phone in the family notices within a few seconds and goes back to the welcome screen.

This cannot be undone. We do not keep backups we could restore it from.

What is left afterwards

  • On the phone: nothing of the family. If the app was added to the home screen, you can delete that icon like any other app. Delete My Data also deletes the phone's anonymous sign-in. After leaving instead, clear the website data for dinnerdecisions.app in your browser's settings to remove it.
  • The anonymous device id: a random id that Firebase gave the browser. Once its phone has left, or its family has been deleted, it is connected to nothing and carries no name or other information. When an id is deleted, Google removes it from its systems within 180 days.
  • Google's own systems may take a short time to finish removing deleted data.

The Privacy Policy has the full details of what is kept and for how long.

Lost access? Ask us to do it

If you can no longer open your family, for example because a phone was lost, reset or replaced, email hello@dinnerdecisions.app and ask us to delete it.

Because there are no accounts, please include enough for us to find the right family and be sure it is yours:

  • the family's name, as it appeared in the app;
  • the invite code, if you still have it, for example in an old message;
  • some of the first names or meals in the family, and roughly when it was started.

We will delete the family, or just the names or votes you ask about, and reply to confirm, within 30 days. We will only use your email to deal with your request.
