Accessibility

Last reviewed: November 17, 2026

Everyone in a household should be able to pick dinner, whatever device they use and however they use it.

Our goal is for Dinner Decisions to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. We have not yet had the app independently audited, so we do not claim that it fully meets them today.

What we do

  • The app follows your phone's light or dark setting, and its colours are chosen for readable contrast in both.
  • It respects your phone's setting to reduce motion.
  • Meals you have picked are shown with a tick and gathered under Your picks, not by colour alone.
  • Every control is a real button or link, and meal tiles tell screen readers whether they are picked.
  • Nothing depends on sound, and nothing has a time limit.
  • Everything in the app is plain text you can read, and the plan and shopping list can be shared as text or printed.

What we know falls short

  • Meal icons. Each meal shows an emoji picked from its name. Screen readers may read the emoji's name aloud as well as the meal's name.
  • Screen reader testing. We have checked the app with screen readers only in part, so some screens may be harder to follow than they should be.

Tell us

If something in Dinner Decisions is hard to use, or you need anything on these pages in another form, please email hello@dinnerdecisions.app.

It helps to know what you were trying to do, the phone or computer and browser you were using, and any assistive technology, such as a screen reader. We read every message, will reply, and will fix what we can.
